Dell Secure Connect Gateway has two critical flaws with the same upgrade destination, including a remote command-execution route that doesn't need a login. That's a useful place to start your Monday.

CVE-2026-61410 is missing authorisation in Secure Connect Gateway 5 point 0: Application versions before 5 point 36 point 00 point 00, and Appliance versions before 5 point 36 point 00 point 16. A specially crafted request can bypass execution restrictions and run commands remotely. CVSS is nine point four: over the network, low complexity, no credentials, no user interaction, unchanged scope, high confidentiality and integrity impact, and low availability impact. Dell says upgrade at the earliest opportunity to those respective versions or later.

The companion, CVE-2026-80238, is unnecessary privilege exposure through the Docker socket, allowing an operator with SSH access to reach root without a password. It affects those same versions and has the same upgrade destination. CVSS is nine point three: local access, low complexity, no privileges or interaction required in the vector, changed scope, and high impact across confidentiality, integrity and availability. There's a mismatch worth keeping: the narrative requires a low-privileged SSH operator, even though the vector says no privileges. Don't mistake this one for the unauthenticated network route.

FreeIPA has a particularly nasty identity chain in CVE-2026-76578. Its self-managed OTP token access-control rule neither requires authentication nor restricts the extra attributes added with a token entry; combined with a separately tracked directory-server access-control evaluation flaw, an anonymous LDAP client can create an attacker-controlled Kerberos principal and put it in the administrators group. It's the visitor badge printer issuing master keys. CVSS is nine point eight: network access, low complexity, no credentials or interaction, unchanged scope, and high impact across all three categories. The affected-product entries name Red Hat Enterprise Linux ten and seven, but give no package range or fix, so those entries alone aren't enough to determine which FreeIPA deployments are vulnerable.

D-Link DIR-822A firmware A underscore 101 has a publicly disclosed exploit for CVE-2026-86296, a stack-based buffer overflow involving strcpy in udhcpcd's serverpacket dot c. CVSS is ten: over the network, low complexity, no credentials or interaction, changed scope, and high confidentiality, integrity and availability impact. The material identifies the vulnerable component but not the exact network exposure conditions or a fixed firmware. Check that precise model and firmware; a perfect score isn't a substitute for knowing which service an attacker can actually reach.

Two public command-injection exploits affect Advantech WISE-6610 firmware 1 point 2 point 1 underscore 20251110. The named models are WISE-6610 with NB, EB, TB, JB and CB suffixes, their EL-prefixed counterparts, and WISE-6610P with DEA, DNA and DTA suffixes. CVE-2026-79697 reaches the Basic Station certificate-deletion handler through its act argument. It's nine point nine: network access, low complexity, low-privileged credentials, no user interaction, changed scope, and high impact across all three categories. No fix is specified for that entry.

CVE-2026-79698 hits the Node-RED Library handler's act argument on the same Advantech models and firmware. This one does name a fix: upgrade to 1 point 2 point 4 underscore 20260821. It's also nine point nine: over the network, low complexity, low privileges, no interaction, changed scope, and high confidentiality, integrity and availability impact. Don't silently extend that fix claim to the certificate-deletion bug; the supplied details don't do that.

Linksys RE7000 firmware 2 point 0 point 15 has public exploit code for CVE-2026-86299. Its PingTest handler passes attacker-controlled IP, packet-size or repeat-count arguments into OS command injection: a diagnostic tool with rather too much initiative. CVSS is nine point nine, reachable over the network with low complexity and low privileges, no user interaction, changed scope, and high impact across confidentiality, integrity and availability. No fixed version is supplied.

The Bahçelievler Municipality BiHayat app has an authentication-bypass finding, CVE-2026-6223, caused by insufficient restriction of repeated authentication attempts. The stated range begins at 2 point 1 point 7 and ends inclusively at the marker zero seven zero nine two zero two six; no fix is given, and the disclosure says the vendor didn't respond. CVSS is nine point four: network access, low complexity, no privileges or interaction, unchanged scope, high confidentiality and integrity impact, and low availability impact. For all these new CVEs, today's details provide no EPSS probabilities, KEV status or deadlines, or ransomware links. That's missing information, not evidence of safety.

Now to the reported attacks, starting with a patch that supersedes yesterday's patch. Every on-premises N-able N-central build below 2026 point 3 point 1 point 14 needs Hotfix Four, including servers updated to Hotfix Three a day earlier. The issue is described as maximum-severity unauthenticated remote code execution, and this is the fourth hotfix in five weeks. N-able's incident notice says it's been exploited; its release notes say exploitation is unconfirmed. Keep that contradiction visible, but don't let it obscure the clear version threshold.

MikroTik RouterOS is also under active attack. Reports describe a chain of two recently disclosed flaws used to take over routers with internet-exposed SSH, while SANS identifies an SSH authentication bypass and says a patch arrived late last week. Neither affected nor fixed versions are supplied here. If you run RouterOS, check the vendor's version guidance and SSH exposure, and investigate exposed devices for compromise rather than treating installation of the patch as the end of the job.

Adobe Commerce and Magento stores are being backdoored through the StyleSmuggler zero-day, which allows code execution. There's no affected range, patch version or detailed exploit mechanism in the supplied report, so watch for those specifics and check store integrity if you operate either platform. Separately, a public TantoSec exploit chains an AES-CBC padding oracle in Telerik UI for ASP.NET AJAX into unauthenticated remote code execution. That chain requires a particular non-default application configuration, and Progress patched it in July; check the configuration prerequisites and July fixes before treating every Telerik deployment as equally exposed.

ScreenConnect has two distinct stories, and they shouldn't be welded together. Researchers describe worm-like activity in which backdoored instances transfer and execute a four-stage VBScript chain on newly connected clients. ConnectWise separately warns of a new Remote Access vulnerability, with temporary mitigations available and a patch planned later this week; the actual mitigation steps and affected versions aren't included here. Check instance and client provenance for the campaign, and consult the vendor's temporary measures for the flaw. Nothing supplied establishes that one caused the other.

On the breach side, Mathspace says attackers stole data belonging to more than a million students, staff and parents after compromising its internal Metabase reporting system. The stolen fields and entry method aren't specified, but the breached component matters: the reporting layer, not necessarily the learning application itself. Trezor's August breach at shipping provider ShipMonk now affects eighty-one thousand customers, after another sixty-seven thousand US customers were identified. That's a logistics-provider exposure, not evidence here of compromised wallets or stolen funds.

One Linux finding is worth a targeted check: North Korean hackers are reported to be embedding a backdoor in HAProxy for long-term surveillance of automotive and media organisations in South Korea. For that target set, examine HAProxy deployment integrity, not just application code behind it. The supplied report doesn't identify the initial-access route or provide detection indicators.

Your priority order: patch Dell's affected gateways and on-premises N-central below Hotfix Four, apply MikroTik's applicable fix, and update the specified Advantech Node-RED flaw. Check exposed RouterOS devices for compromise, ScreenConnect instances and clients for tampering, and Telerik's configuration prerequisites. Watch for ScreenConnect's promised patch, precise StyleSmuggler guidance, and missing firmware and package fixes. 522 AM, brought to you by Lab522. hello at lab522 dot co dot uk.
