Good morning, it's 5:22 AM. Saturday, the seventeenth of October, twenty twenty-six.

Five minutes was enough to turn a backup tool into a backdoor delivery service. Google Threat Intelligence and Mandiant, alongside ManageEngine's threat lab, have detailed tampering with AOMEI's own update servers between April and July. Backupper is Windows backup and disk-cloning software; it trusts two unsigned text files telling it what to download and run. The download's checksum lives in the same unsigned instructions. Change both, and the checksum proves precisely nothing useful.

Attackers tracked as UNC7017 changed those files for five to ten minutes, then restored them. Clients polling inside a window received the CROSSROAD downloader and SHINYROAD backdoor; everyone else got genuine files. A signed Radmin binary loaded the malicious DLL. That's a clever way to make the next investigator's download look reassuringly clean. AOMEI says it corrected its servers in July, but ManageEngine hasn't independently confirmed containment, and that doesn't clean infected endpoints. If you run Backupper or AnyViewer, hunt across April to July: look for the RadminComponentRuntime scheduled task and unexpected execution from Backupper's HTML pop-up folder. Preserve cached files and check what network logs show at the original alert time. Google has YARA rules; ManageEngine has Sigma rules. I'd reopen dismissed alerts here. Even current Backupper 8 point 5 point 0 has no changelog entry announcing stronger update verification.

And on fixes that need checking, a correction to yesterday's BeyondTrust timing: the Remote Support patch shipped on the eighth of October, not at disclosure; SaaS was patched by the ninth. CISA has now added CVE-2026-102764 to KEV, with Monday the nineteenth as the federal deadline. For self-hosted Remote Support, apply BT26-05-RS to versions 24 point 3 point 1 through 26 point 2 point 3; older builds need upgrading first. Self-hosted Privileged Remote Access shares the affected code and needs its own patch too. Shadowserver sees more than fourteen hundred exposed Remote Support instances, though that isn't a count of vulnerable machines. Check the installed patch rather than the closed change ticket, and restrict the administrative interfaces to trusted networks.

That Monday federal deadline also now applies to the Fortinet flaw behind PEERSTITCH, CVE-2025-61624, patched back in April: fixed FortiOS branches start at 7 point 4 point 10 or 7 point 6 point 5; FortiPAM needs 1 point 7 point 1, FortiProxy 7 point 4 point 12 or 7 point 6 point 5, and FortiSwitchManager 7 point 0 point 7. Meanwhile, Zscaler has shown how attackers exploit NetScaler's previously mentioned CVE-2026-88771, unauthenticated command execution requiring no optional features: they stage payloads in HTTP access logs using User-Agent values beginning INDEX colon followed by Base64. Snapshot and collect forensic evidence before upgrading, because CISA warns updates can erase visibility; use Zscaler's published hunt instructions afterwards.

Away from gateways, the Commerce part of Wednesday's Adobe batch now has a sharper warning: Sansec calls CVE-2026-103849 BlockStrap, found four potential paths to arbitrary PHP execution within twenty-four hours, and expects mass attacks, though none have been seen. I'd move that ahead of Adobe's thirty-day priority: apply the twenty twenty-six October release or isolated patch, not just a plain Composer install, and retain September's VULN-39341 hotfix. For another application with a working exploit, Rapid7's GitLab CI/CD regex-overflow proof of concept runs commands as the git operating-system account, but requires an attacker to push malicious project content before the unauthenticated lint request. Patch self-managed CE and EE for CVE-2026-97229 to 19 point 4 point 2, or the branch fixes 19 point 3 point 5 and 19 point 2 point 8.

There's no patch for the next interruption: a cybersecurity incident at BAE Systems has stopped paper and electronic bankruptcy notices for every US federal bankruptcy court since the ninth of October, with no restoration estimate. If you support a bankruptcy practice, arrange alternative notice checks; filings remain open and registered attorneys' electronic filing notifications still work. Back in the deployment queue, Microsoft's Thursday follow-up adds more than thirty fixes, including two dozen Office remote-code-execution flaws, six reachable through the Preview Pane; Microsoft says none has been exploited as a zero-day. Add these to October's rollout, including the updated Mac Office builds, and pick up the ASP.NET Core fixes alongside Identity Web and MSAL for Node.js.

I'd give the on-call team room for evidence collection before the emergency upgrades. Keep those two jobs on the same ticket so neither disappears at handover. Have a good Saturday.
